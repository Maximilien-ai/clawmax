## Event dependency audit exception

[Initial source CI](https://github.com/Maximilien-ai/clawmax/actions/runs/44248938319) stopped at the high/critical audit gate. The advisory in
[GHSA-fc5q-39xc-vjpf](https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-fc5q-39xc-vjpf)
covers Picomatch through 4.0.7. The npm registry currently reports 4.0.7 as
latest, and the advisory lists no patched release.

- `npm audit --json` reports 574 total dependencies in the metadata, nine
  moderate findings, 13 high entries, and zero critical. CI traced its
  high entries to the Picomatch advisory. All three affected Picomatch
  lockfile locations are marked as development dependencies.
- `npm audit --omit=dev --json` reports three moderate and zero high or
  critical findings for the Dashboard production tree.
- The Dashboard Dockerfile installs only production dependencies in the
  runtime stage. Inspection of the RC99-dirty container found two separate
  Picomatch 4.0.4 copies, one in the system npm tool and one in the npm tool
  bundled with OpenClaw. Runtime exposure through those tools is unresolved.
- The owner approved the exception for the four-hour event. It applies only to
  this advisory on the RC100 branch and tag, requires that the affected lockfile
  entries are dev-only, and expires November 20 at 00:00 UTC. Production
  high/critical findings and all other high/critical advisories still fail.
- The live audit passed in RC100 context and rejected the same advisory in
  RC101 context. The Dashboard team owns the follow-up. RC101 requires a
  verified patch or other remediation for the affected dependency and an
  assessment of the two npm-bundled copies before publication.
