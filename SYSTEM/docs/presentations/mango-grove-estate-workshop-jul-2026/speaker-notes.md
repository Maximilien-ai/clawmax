# ClawMax.ai x Mango Grove Estate Workshop #1

## Slide 1 — Opening

- Welcome everyone to Mango Grove Estate and frame this as a hands-on workshop, not a polished keynote.
- State the core topic plainly: building and orchestrating AI agent teams that can run real workflows.
- Position the session around moving beyond one-shot chatbot interactions into persistent, operational systems.
- Mention the QR code as the easiest way for people to keep ClawMax.ai handy for after the demo.
- Practical cue: use the first screen to let people settle, scan, and understand the scope of the workshop before you move into the technical framing.

## Slide 2 — The AI Agent Shift

- Explain the shift from prompt-response tools to independent agents that can hold state, perform multi-step work, and operate over time.
- Emphasize that the interesting problem is no longer just model quality; it is workflow design, coordination, memory, and reliability.
- Introduce OpenClaw as the open-source runtime layer that makes persistent, self-hosted agents possible.
- Make the transition: if OpenClaw is the runtime engine, ClawMax.ai is the operating and orchestration layer around it.
- Practical cue: frame this as a systems and operations discussion, not just an AI novelty discussion.

## Slide 3 — Orchestration With ClawMax.ai

- Walk through the idea of one interface for building, monitoring, and managing teams of agents.
- Highlight the practical value: visible workflows, visible handoffs, visible progress, and easier operations.
- Explain that orchestration is what makes agent systems usable by teams instead of only by people comfortable living in terminals and config files.
- Set up the live portion by telling the room you will move from concepts into actual workflows and real use cases.
- Practical cue: this is the cleanest bridge from OpenClaw internals to the ClawMax operating layer.

## Slide 4 — Live Co-Creation and Q&A

- Invite the room to provide real business or personal workflow examples.
- Explain that the goal is to translate those examples into operational agent workflows live, including roles, stages, and handoffs.
- Use this slide to keep the session interactive rather than purely presentational.
- Close by offering implementation detail in the Q&A: deployment, workflow design, self-hosting, ClawMax.ai setup, and first-use patterns.
- Practical cue: end with one workflow people can picture using tomorrow, not just a general statement about AI agents.

## Optional Closing

- Autonomous AI becomes useful when it is structured, observable, and operational.
- OpenClaw gives the runtime foundation.
- ClawMax.ai gives the orchestration layer that helps real people and teams use it.
- The best next step is to start with one real workflow and make it visible end to end.
